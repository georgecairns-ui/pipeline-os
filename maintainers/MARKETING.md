# Marketing notes: Pipeline OS

## In one sentence

After every sales call, Pipeline OS updates the deal for you, tells you what to ask next time and drafts the email back, so nothing slips and you get better at selling.

## Before and after, for the owner

**Before:** deals live in your head, your inbox and a spreadsheet nobody updates. You finish a sales call, mean to write it up and send a follow-up, and by Thursday you've forgotten what they said. You chase the wrong people, spend weeks on deals that were never going to happen, and miss the other problem the prospect mentioned that you could have solved. Maybe you pay for a CRM you barely open.

**After:** you paste 3 rough lines after a call (or your recorder does it for you) and a minute later the deal is up to date: what happened, what you still don't know, the questions to ask next time, a tip on how the call went, and the email back, written in your voice. Every deal is on one board with what it's worth. Chases are drafted before you think of them, and you're prepped the day before every sales call. You press send.

Time saved per call and per week: to check. Do not quote a figure until it has been measured with real users.

## 3 things to show in a 60-second video

1. **Notes in, deal out.** Open a deal, paste 3 messy lines from a call, press Update the deal. Cut to a minute later: the deal check fills in, the next steps appear, the email back is drafted. Press Draft: it opens in Gmail, ready to send.
2. **The deal check.** Show the 8 questions marked Known, Partly known and Not known yet, with what the prospect actually said, and the "Ask next time" list. "It tells you which deals are real, and what to ask to find out."
3. **The board that runs itself.** The pipeline by stage with the values, a "Claude moved this" card with Keep and Undo, and Review with a chase drafted for a deal that went quiet.
